🚀 IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

1. Open your browser: http://localhost:5174

2. Upload Excel Price List:
   - Click "Upload Price List"
   - Select: sample_price_list.xlsx
   - Click "Upload File"

3. Generate Quotation:
   - Fill "Customer Name"
   - Select vehicle models (autocomplete available)
   - Set quantities
   - Click "Generate Quotation"

4. Print Quotation:
   - Click "📄 Print / Save as PDF"
   - Save as PDF or print
